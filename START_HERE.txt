MYSURU MITHRA — START HERE

1. Unzip this folder.
2. Open config.js and add your Supabase Project URL + Publishable/anon key.
3. In Supabase SQL Editor, run supabase_setup.sql.
4. Create your team/admin email + password in Supabase Authentication.
5. Host the WHOLE folder. The QR code must point to the hosted index.html.
6. Test one rating on index.html.
7. Open admin.html on your phone and log in — all feedback records will be there.
8. Use qr-maker.html after hosting: paste your public index.html URL and generate the QR.

Do not put a Supabase secret/service_role key in config.js.
Keep all files/folders together, including stickers/.
